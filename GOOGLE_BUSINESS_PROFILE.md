## Google Business Profile Optimization Tips

### For All 3 Profiles

1. **Verify immediately** — verify by postcard, phone, or email. Unverified profiles don't show in search.

2. **Photos matter most** — businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests. Upload 10+ photos for each business. Add new photos weekly.

3. **Post weekly** — Google rewards active profiles. Post offers, updates, or tips once a week.

4. **Collect reviews** — ask every customer to leave a review. Reply to all reviews within 24 hours. Target: 10+ reviews in first month.

5. **Keep info accurate** — wrong hours or phone number kills trust. Update immediately if anything changes.

6. **Use keywords in description** — naturally include your target keywords (city names, product types, service names).

7. **Add products/services with prices** — profiles with prices get more clicks.

8. **Enable messaging** — let customers message you directly from Google. Respond within 1 hour.

9. **Q&A section** — pre-populate with common questions (shown above). Don't wait for customers to ask.

10. **Google Maps photos** — upload geotagged photos taken at your business location. Helps with local ranking.

### Review Request Template

After a customer purchase or service completion, send this via WhatsApp:

```
Hi [name], thanks for choosing [business name]!

If you had a good experience, would you take 30 seconds to leave us a Google review? It really helps other customers find us.

[Google Review Link]

Thank you!
```

To get your Google Review link: Google Business Profile -> Home -> "Ask for reviews" -> Copy link.
